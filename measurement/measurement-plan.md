# Measurement plan

Source of truth is native Instagram Insights plus site analytics filtered on `utm_campaign=hub-nov26`. Never estimate or invent a number; leave a cell blank if it isn't available. Log each post in `tracker.csv` 24 hours and 7 days after publishing.

## Metrics, in order of importance
| Metric | Why | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Sends per reach | Strongest discovery signal | Rising across posts means the hook and topic travel |
| Non-follower reach % | Is discovery working | Reels should beat carousels here |
| Saves | Reference value | Carousels and templates should lead |
| Avg watch time and 3-second hold (Reels) | Hook quality | Low hold means rewrite the first 3 seconds |
| Profile visits, follows | Profile conversion | Many views and few visits is a hook-to-profile gap |
| Link clicks and site visits (UTM) | The campaign goal | Compare by `utm_content` |
| Academy and account sign-ups, community applications | Outcome | Check with the site admin views |

## Baselines and targets
No baseline exists. Use D01 to D03 as the baseline. At the Day 7 review, set targets for D08 to D14 from the median of D01 to D07, not from external benchmarks.

## Test design (one variable at a time)
| Test | Posts | Compare |
|---|---|---|
| Format for reach | Reels D03, D07, D08, D10 vs carousels | Non-follower reach, sends per reach |
| Text card vs carousel | D02, D09 vs D01, D06 | Sends per reach, saves |
| Hook mechanism | D03 contrarian, D07 founder, D08 question, D10 curiosity | 3-second hold, watch time |
| Timing | 12:30 vs 18:30 | Reach in the first 2 hours |

Small samples are noisy: one post is an anecdote. Judge patterns across at least three posts of a type.

## Debrief template (Day 14)
1. What worked (top 3 posts by sends per reach and by site visits).
2. What didn't, and the likely stage (reach, profile conversion, or site conversion).
3. What to repeat, drop and test next.
4. Follow-on plan for the next 14 days.
