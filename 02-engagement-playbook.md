# Engagement and distribution playbook

## Daily routine (about 30 minutes)
1. **Before posting (10 min):** open the brief, check the facts flag, check the live page, load the tracked link.
2. **Post, then first comment within 1 minute** (from the brief). Add hashtags in the first comment if not in the caption.
3. **First hour (10 min):** answer every comment. Reply to each DM keyword with the link. Early replies lift signals.
4. **Stories (5 min):** run the three-frame sequence in `stories/stories-plan.md`.
5. **Outreach (5 min):** leave 5 thoughtful comments on posts from AI governance, AI tooling or insurance-technology accounts (no pitching, add a specific fact or a question).

## Reply templates
Keep replies specific; do not paste these word for word.

- **Keyword DM:** "Here's the [guide / template / calculator]: {tracked link}. Which part are you working on? I'll point you to the right page."
- **Compliance question:** "Short version: it depends on the risk tier. The framework page lists the status and dates: {link}. General information, not legal advice."
- **Tool-choice question:** "What's the use case and the budget? I'll point you to the closest comparison: {compare link}."
- **Disagreement:** "Fair challenge. Which source are you looking at? If it's more current than ours, send it and we'll check it against the two-source rule."
- **Correction:** "Thank you, checking it now. Corrections go to {site}/contribute and are applied once verified."

## Collaboration and cross-posting
- Add an Instagram collab invite on D04 and D07 to two relevant accounts (AI governance, AI education, Swiss tech). Only invite accounts you know; no mass outreach.
- Resurface each carousel on LinkedIn as a PDF document the next morning (same slides, longer caption). Reuse text cards as LinkedIn image posts. Facebook can take the Reels.
- Do not reuse Reels with third-party watermarks.

## Moderation
Remove spam and abuse; do not delete honest criticism. Answer factual challenges in public once, politely.

## Weekly checkpoints
- **Day 7 (Tue 10 Nov):** compare D01 to D07 against each other (see `measurement/measurement-plan.md`), pick the best format and hook mechanism, set week-2 targets.
- **Day 14 (Tue 17 Nov):** write a one-page debrief and decide what continues. Candidate follow-on: a weekly format fed by the in-app social generator.
